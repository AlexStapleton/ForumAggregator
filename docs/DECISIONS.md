| D-111 | **Known devices, so spam can't lock the owner out** (OWASP's "device cookies"). D-110's per-account limits meant anyone could make the owner wait by spamming wrong passwords. So attackers, who don't have the cookie, still hit the account-wide limits, and the owner's known devices aren't locked by them. Up to 20 known devices per account (least recently used dropped). Changing the password forgets every known device but the current one. Logging out keeps the cookie (the browser stays known). Limit: a brand-new browser during an attack waits like the attacker | User ("Why would login be blocked if someone is spamming?
